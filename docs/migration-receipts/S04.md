## Public read audit

- **Task events.** Before S04, no ctl command paged a standalone task's events. MCP
  `get_task_events` (seq) and `get_task_event_page` (cursor) existed. The cursor
  reader was newest-first and backward only. It had a v1 cursor, no generation or
  expiry, `INVALID_PARAMS` strings without stable codes, no gap, drain or change
  evidence, and no no-follow open. Its default filter excluded `assistant_delta`.
  The existing `ctl-event-page*.json` fixtures are the seq-based node activity
  shape (`workflow-inspect --view activity`).
- **Writer facts the new read depends on.** `seq` is contiguous over written lines.
  An unserializable event is dropped without consuming a seq. An I/O failure
  disables the log with no persisted marker. Terminal metadata, including
  `output_drained`, is persisted before `task_finished`. When drained,
  `task_finished` is followed synchronously by close. Retention deletes whole
  tasks, 30 days by default.
- **Title and prompt.** The title is in `status`, `status-batch` and `task-list-page`.
  No public ctl read returned the prompt: the record holds a 2000-character
  preview plus a task-bound 0600 sidecar. HarflowMac reads title, prompt and live
  activity from `events.jsonl` directly (S02).
- **Changes.** The terminal `outcome` exposes raw porcelain text, base and head
  commits, `new_commits` and raw `committed_diff_stat` text. There are no
  structured per-file counts, no diff text and no file content. Artifacts are
  metadata only.
- **History.** `task-list-page`, `task-list-compact` and `workflow-list-page` are
  keyset-paged with `has_more`, `history_incomplete`, `counts_complete` and
  `bootstrap_pending`. Successor discovery (`predecessor_task_id`) is MCP-only.
- **Statistics.** See G-STATS below.

## Delivered contract

Specification: `docs/contracts.md`, section "Task event pages and prompts".

- **`harflow-ctl task-events TASK_ID [--from newest|oldest | --cursor C] [--limit N]
  [--kinds K | --all-kinds] --json`**, capability `task-event-page.v1`.
  - **Callers.** Direct callers only. The caller gate runs before id resolution,
    followed by the guarded resolution `status` uses.
  - **Pages.** Pages are ascending. Cursors bound the consumed byte span and come
    as `older_cursor` and `newer_cursor`; a client polls by re-issuing
    `newer_cursor`.
  - **Cursor format.** The cursor is opaque base64url v2, generation `py-events-1`,
    at most 2048 characters. It is bound to the task, file identity, a
    length-encoded head anchor and a boundary anchor. It carries a neighbour seq and
    a `skip` bit, so a gap at a page boundary can be attributed.
  - **Limits.** `--limit` 1..200 (default 100) decoded lines; scan at most 1 MiB;
    events at most 16384 bytes (existing stub); the whole document at most 262144
    bytes, trimmed by direction.
  - **Accounting.** `span`, `skipped {malformed, oversized}` (oversized counted per
    window) and `gaps` (seqs that no parsed line accounts for).
  - **`log` state.** One of:
    - `open`
    - `settling`: drained, `task_finished` not yet present, 30 s grace
    - `final`: drained, `task_finished` at physical EOF
    - `incomplete`
    - `unknown`
    - `unavailable`

    It is evaluated from capture state after a record re-read. `final` does not
    prove complete history. `incomplete` may still become `final`.
  - **`change_token`.** Equality of sampled storage fingerprints and computed
    lifecycle only.
  - **Refusals.** `event_cursor_invalid` and `event_cursor_expired`, with
    `error.data.reload: "newest"`. Python has no wall-clock TTL, and any engine may
    expire cursors; Rust cursors are a new generation. Existing `task_pending_launch`,
    unknown-task and direct-caller refusals apply.
  - **Open tasks.** Polling an open task does not replay the raw stream log; the
    recorded status is reported.
- **`harflow-ctl task-prompt TASK_ID [--max-bytes N] --json`**, capability `task-prompt.v1`.
  - **Callers.** Direct callers only.
  - **States.** `available`, `preview_only` (non-authoritative), `integrity_failed`
    and `unavailable`.
  - **Integrity.** `verified`, `unverified`, `failed` or `not_applicable`. The
    sidecar is verified up to 4 MiB; above that, a bounded prefix is read and the
    result is `unverified`.
  - **Bytes.** `--max-bytes` 1..131072 (default 65536) bounds the UTF-8 encoding of
    the returned text, cut at a character boundary. Interior invalid bytes become
    U+FFFD. JSON escaping can make the document larger than 262144 bytes.
  - **Reading.** The sidecar is task-bound and opened no-follow as a regular file.
  - **Fields.** `total_bytes`, `returned_bytes`, `truncated`, `complete`.
- **Reconnect and history rules** (G-HISTORY), documented from the implemented
  behaviour.
- **`task-changes`**: proposed, not implemented (G-DIFF, below).

Implementation:
- New modules `event_pages.py`, `task_event_read.py`, `task_prompt_read.py` and

- `ctl.py` gains the two subparsers, the caller gate and the dispatch.

## Reviews

**Plan loop.** Two rounds, with Opus, Codex (gpt-6-astra, low) and Vibe.

- Round 1: all three requested changes. Their findings converged on these points:
  - the race between terminal persistence and `task_finished` (leading to the
    `settling` state);
  - false gaps from the default `assistant_delta` exclusion;
  - forward and newer cursor semantics and progress through oversized lines;
  - the whole-document budget;
  - the scope of the change token;
  - authority and no-follow path safety;
  - ownership of `version-info.json`;
  - prompt integrity and bounds;
  - G-DIFF hardening, budget and provenance.

  All were accepted. Rejected or modified:
  - Vibe's proposal to suppress gaps under any filter was replaced by accounting
    for filtered seqs.
  - Vibe's mtime-churn concern was rejected: a spurious refresh is the safe
    direction.
  - Codex's full consistency/retry protocol for the token was not adopted. The
    token is a refresh hint, and the record is re-read after the log.
- Round 2: Opus and Vibe approved. Codex requested changes:
  - consumed-span cursors;
  - restored limits;
  - token wording;
  - head-anchor extent;
  - grace as a heuristic.

  These, plus Opus's and Vibe's minors (G-DIFF disposable git dir, trim
  direction, the EOF read bound, the caller gate before resolution, refusal
  fixtures, gap attribution across pages, the post-settlement capture budget), were
  folded in. The owner approved proceeding without another round.

**Code loop.** Two rounds, with the same three reviewers started fresh.

- Round 1. All findings were accepted and fixed, each with a regression test:
  - Opus: one blocker. A backward page dropped a valid line before an oversized
    line whose length is not a window multiple.
  - Opus: one major. Polling an open task replayed the raw stream log.
  - Codex: malformed cursor fields escaped the stable refusal; a deeply nested EOF
    line crashed the finality check.
  - Minors from all three reviewers:
    - cross-page gap attribution;
    - `has_newer` with partial tails;
    - the empty older page's `newer_cursor`;
    - seq inheritance;
    - a looping pread;
    - clock skew;
    - wire version constant;
    - the duplicate record read;
    - prompt UTF-8 cutting;
    - integrity `failed`;
    - doc wording;
    - managed-caller and native-id tests.
- Round 2.
  - Opus approved.
  - Codex raised two minors: `has_newer` probe exhaustion, and replacement
    decoding exceeding `--max-bytes`. Both were accepted and fixed.
  - Vibe: no verdict. Its round-2 run stalled with no output for about 20 minutes
    after a programmatic tool-approval denial and was cancelled. Its round-1
    verdict was APPROVE, and its round-1 minors were fixed. Under the workflow's
    round-2 rule (only minor findings remain), no further round was run.
