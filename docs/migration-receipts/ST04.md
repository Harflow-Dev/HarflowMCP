## Independently reviewed restriction

Two design-review rounds approved a restricted foundation under existing policy: one exact root
headless Fresh connected worker launch, its queued unissued steering response, and a linked retry
of the same assignment/node/visit/configured candidate. The source must have a positive finite cap
and a supporting adapter. No alternate graph, assignment, backend, permission, Native, Resume,
specialist or nested invocation authority is added. Already issued work refuses before withdrawal;
a race after accepted withdrawal stays unknown and never authorizes replacement.

A separate generation-bound withdraw-only protocol is required. The legacy task-ID cancellation
marker can trigger a signal after spawn and is therefore unsuitable. Withdrawal is not permission
for a late kill. Ordinary cancellation keeps its existing behavior and remains a separate action.
The caller/Monitor routes compose actual existing control authorization with steering authority;
human verification alone does not permit controlling an active caller-owned tree.

An exact owner acknowledgment must prove never-started settlement and matching resource/message
release. Intent, source/response fences and recoverable release journal precede replacement.
The queued response's issuance charge survives revocation. The original accepted decision,
checklist, siblings, artifacts and worktree effects remain intact. A linked activation/task identity
is published atomically before dispatch. Existing attempt calculations, grants and configured caps
remain authoritative; no new allowance is invented.

The old execution retains its permanent acceptance fence. Delayed callbacks cannot finish the
replacement token, overwrite certified no-start status, alter accounting, or publish stale session
or attention state. A withdrawn original task ID cannot be reused as a new launch. A preparing
replacement identity may be recovered only with positive no-issuance proof; unknown or requested
spawn identities are never replaced.

Poststart containment/replacement and alternate graph selection remain material unaccepted modes.
No best-effort cancellation result, empty survivor list, manual resource release, owner death or
TaskRecord absence alone substitutes for the required proof.
